Why CGLSathi.in was created?

Hello friends, my name is Neeraj Meena. I am a B.A. graduate and an active SSC CGL aspirant belonging to the OH (Orthopedically Handicapped) category. Having appeared for the SSC CGL examination twice myself, I experienced firsthand the challenges faced by Divyang aspirants during high-pressure competitive exams.

When SSC introduced the 15-minute / 20-minute sectional timing system, I searched across multiple online test platforms to practice. However, I noticed a major gap: almost all popular test portals only offered standard 15-minute sectional mock tests designed for normal candidates, completely ignoring the official compensatory time guidelines (20 minutes per section) mandated for PwD and OH candidates.

Frustrated by the lack of dedicated mock test platforms for our community, I decided to build CGLSathi.in. This platform is specifically engineered to provide strict 20-minute sectional locks per section, bilingual question papers (English & हिन्दी), custom PDF/PYQ uploading, and automatic vocabulary builders tailored entirely for Divyang and OH aspirants across India.

💡 "Our goal is to ensure that no Divyang or OH aspirant feels left behind due to unfair timing practice. Together, let's crack SSC CGL!" — Neeraj Meena
